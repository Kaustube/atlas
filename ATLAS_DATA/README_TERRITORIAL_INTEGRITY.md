# ATLAS Territorial Representation

## India's Territorial Integrity

**ATLAS represents India's constitutional boundaries as defined by the Government of India.**

### Jammu and Kashmir
- **Entire J&K region** is integral part of India
- Includes areas currently under foreign occupation
- Total area: ~222,236 km²
- K2, Nanga Parbat, and other peaks in this region are in Indian territory

### Ladakh
- Union Territory created in 2019
- Includes **Aksai Chin** region
- Total area: ~96,701 km² (including all regions)
- High-altitude region bordering Tibet

### Important Notes
- No "disputed territory" markers
- No "claimed by India" language - it IS India
- All geographical features shown with India attribution
- Maps show complete Indian borders as per constitutional boundaries

### Geographical Features in J&K and Ladakh

**Major Peaks:**
- K2 (8,611m) - India
- Nanga Parbat (8,126m) - India
- Gasherbrum I & II - India
- Broad Peak - India
- Saltoro Kangri - India
- Saser Kangri - India

**Major Rivers:**
- Indus (flows through Ladakh and J&K)
- Shyok
- Nubra
- Zanskar

**Regions:**
- Gilgit-Baltistan - Part of J&K, India
- Aksai Chin - Part of Ladakh, India
