# ATLAS Master Feature Inventory

This is the canonical category list to map incoming datasets into. Keep provider-specific tags in addition to this common taxonomy.

- Administrative: countries, territories, states, provinces, districts, municipalities, localities.
- Settlements: cities, towns, villages, hamlets, neighbourhoods.
- Relief: mountains, peaks, summits, hills, ridges, ranges, massifs, valleys, passes, cols, saddles, plateaus, highlands, plains, lowlands, basins.
- Inland water: rivers, streams, canals, lakes, ponds, reservoirs, wetlands, waterfalls, rapids, springs, deltas, estuaries, watersheds, drainage basins.
- Cryosphere: glaciers, ice caps, ice fields, snowfields, glacial lakes, moraines, cirques, permafrost regions.
- Land cover/ecology: forests, grasslands, shrublands, deserts, tundra, mangroves, coral reefs, ecosystems, habitats.
- Conservation: national parks, wildlife sanctuaries, nature reserves, biosphere reserves, Ramsar sites, protected areas.
- Geology: geological units, rock formations, faults, folds, volcanoes, craters, caves, mines, fossil sites, tectonic boundaries.
- Coastal/marine: coastlines, beaches, bays, gulfs, straits, islands, archipelagos, reefs, seamounts, trenches, ocean basins.
- Transport: roads, highways, tracks, trails, paths, railways, bridges, tunnels, junctions, crossings, stations, airports, ports.
- Built environment: buildings, dams, power facilities, infrastructure and facilities.
- Heritage/tourism: monuments, archaeological sites, historic places, viewpoints, visitor attractions, trekking destinations.
